Privacy Notice

This notice explains how Kolosage Solutions handles enquiries through its website and contact channels. Last updated: 11 November 2026.

The quote form asks for your name or business name, email address, selected service and an optional project brief. This website has no form database. When you submit the form, the details are included in a WhatsApp click-to-chat link to 072 025 5622 and appear as a draft message. WhatsApp receives the link request; the message is sent only if you choose Send in WhatsApp. You can also choose to contact the business through Gmail, phone or Facebook.

Third-party services

WhatsApp, Google/Gmail, mobile and internet providers, and Facebook are independent services. Their own privacy terms apply when you use them, and they may process or retain information under their policies. Please review those policies. Do not include passwords, payment details, identity numbers or other sensitive personal information in an enquiry.

How enquiries are used and kept

Kolosage Solutions uses enquiries to respond, discuss requested services, prepare quotes and provide agreed work. Access should be limited to the business owner and people who need the information to handle the enquiry or service. The proposed practice is to delete or anonymise enquiries that do not become customers within 12 months of the last contact, unless the person asks for continued follow-up or a legal requirement applies. Customer, contract and accounting records may need to be kept for applicable legal periods. WhatsApp and Gmail may retain their own copies under their policies.

Website data and cookies

This page does not include analytics, advertising trackers, or a form-submission database. The website host may process basic technical request data to deliver and protect the site; its processing and retention depend on the hosting provider.

Your questions or requests

To ask about, correct or request deletion of an enquiry held by Kolosage Solutions, contact sangekolose@gmail.com. Requests are subject to applicable legal record-keeping requirements. For information held by WhatsApp, Google or Facebook, contact that service directly.

The 12-month enquiry period above is a recommended business practice and should be followed consistently. This plain-language notice is not legal advice; seek South African privacy advice if you need a formal compliance review.
